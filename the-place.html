Above Ullsfjord, a natural ridge of rocks, moss, and scattered groups of trees stretches out—offering a magnificent panoramic view of the Lyngen Alps, with peaks reaching up to 1,800 meters. The idea to build a series of exclusive panoramic chalets here and turn this location into a destination for international guests came from investor Norman Vogel. He started visiting the region every year in 2015 and has been spending half of each year in Sjursnes since 2022. Davvi Ridge currently consists of six chalets and has additional space for future growth.
