Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

01 Who is responsible

Responsible for processing personal data is Sommarbukt Utvikling AS, Stordalstrandvegen 285, 9030 Sjursnes, Norway, the company behind DAVVI (email: hello@davvi-ridge.com). Please send any questions about data protection to this address. As Norway is part of the European Economic Area, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies.

This privacy policy covers this website (www.davvi-ridge.com) and our booking website booking.davvi-ridge.com, as well as bookings you make with us through Booking.com or Airbnb.

02 Visiting our websites

When you open one of our websites, your browser automatically transmits technical data to the hosting provider: IP address, date and time of access, page requested, referring page, browser type and operating system. This data is needed to deliver the websites securely and is stored in server log files for a short period only. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in secure, working websites (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Hosting of this website: Vercel Inc., 440 N Barranca Ave #4133, Covina, CA 91723, USA. Vercel may process data in the USA; transfers are based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses. The booking website is hosted by Lodgify (see section 04).

Visitor statistics: to understand how this website is used, for example which pages are visited and how often the booking buttons and the availability search are used, we use Vercel Web Analytics, a service of Vercel Inc. (address above). It works without cookies and does not store anything in your browser. With every page view and every click on a booking button or on “Search”, Vercel records the page, the referring page, the time, browser, operating system, device type and the approximate location (country, region, city) derived from your IP address. To tell visits apart, Vercel creates a temporary identifier from the request, which is discarded after 24 hours. The data is only used for aggregated statistics, cannot be traced back to you and is not combined with other data. We keep the statistics for up to 12 months. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving our website and our booking offer (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time by email; you can also block the statistics with a content blocker in your browser.

03 Fonts

Both websites use fonts from Adobe Fonts (Adobe Systems Software Ireland Ltd., 4–6 Riverwalk, Citywest Business Campus, Dublin 24, Ireland). To display them, your browser loads the fonts from Adobe's servers, which transmits your IP address to Adobe. Adobe may transfer data to Adobe Inc. in the USA on the basis of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Adobe Fonts does not set cookies for this purpose. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a consistent appearance of our websites (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

04 Availability search and booking

The availability search on this website and our booking website are provided by Lodgify (Lodgify SL, Barcelona, Spain). When this website loads, the search bar is loaded from Lodgify's servers, which transmits your IP address and browser data to Lodgify. The dates and number of guests you enter are passed on to the booking website, where all bookings are made.

When you book, we process your travel dates, number of guests, contact details and billing details to confirm and manage your booking and to issue your invoice. Payments are handled by the payment provider connected to the booking system; we do not see or store your full card details. Lodgify processes this data on our behalf and is contractually bound to protect it. More details are in Lodgify's own privacy policy.

Legal basis: performance of the rental contract and steps prior to it (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our accounting obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

05 Booking.com and Airbnb

If you book a stay with us through Booking.com or Airbnb, the platform passes your booking details to us: your name, travel dates, number of guests and the contact details the platform shares. We use them to prepare and carry out your stay. The platforms' own privacy policies apply to the data they collect themselves. Legal basis: performance of the rental contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

06 Online check-in

Before you arrive, we ask you to complete an online check-in. We use it to prepare your stay and to meet the guest registration duty under Norwegian law. We ask for your full name, date of birth, nationality, home address, the type and number of your passport or ID card with an upload of the document, your expected arrival time and your phone number.

Accommodation providers in Norway must keep a register of all overnight guests and show it to the police on request. For guests who are not citizens of a Nordic country, this includes passport or ID details. Passport data is stored securely, is only accessible to authorised persons and is used for no other purpose.

Legal basis: legal obligation under section 20 of the Norwegian Immigration Regulations, Utlendingsforskriften (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR), and performance of the rental contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

07 Maps

The chalet pages of the booking website show the location on a map from Google Maps (Google Ireland Ltd., Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland), embedded by Lodgify. When the map loads, your IP address and browser data are transmitted to Google. Google may transfer data to Google LLC in the USA on the basis of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in showing you where the chalets are (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

08 Contacting us by email

If you email us, we process the data you send us (usually name, email address, travel dates and your message) to answer your enquiry. Legal basis: steps prior to a contract or our legitimate interest in answering your enquiry (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

09 Cookies

This website itself does not set cookies and does not use advertising or tracking tools. The visitor statistics described in section 02 work without cookies. The Lodgify search bar may store strictly necessary information in your browser (for example the selected dates) so that the search works.

The booking website only uses cookies that are needed for it to work, for example to remember your currency or to keep a booking in progress. They are set by the booking system and are always active. We do not use cookies for statistics or advertising, and we do not build user profiles. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; strictly necessary cookies do not require consent.

10 Service providers

We do not sell data. We only share it with service providers we need to run our websites and handle bookings, and all of them are contractually bound to protect your data: Vercel (hosting and visitor statistics of this website, USA), Adobe (fonts, Ireland/USA), Lodgify (booking system, booking website and availability search, Spain), Google (maps on the booking website, Ireland/USA), the payment provider connected to the booking system, Booking.com and Airbnb (bookings made on these platforms, Netherlands/Ireland) and Fiken (invoicing and accounting, Norway).

For transfers to the USA we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on Standard Contractual Clauses of the European Commission.

11 How long we keep data

We only keep data for as long as necessary: messages and enquiries until the matter is settled and then for up to 3 years; booking and billing data for up to 5 years after your stay (Norwegian accounting rules); check-in data and the guest register for at least 2 years (Norwegian law); technical server data only as long as needed for secure operation; anonymous visitor statistics for up to 12 months.

12 Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing of your data, to data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where you have given consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Just send us an email to hello@davvi-ridge.com.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet, Postboks 458 Sentrum, 0105 Oslo, Norway, www.datatilsynet.no) or with the supervisory authority in your country of residence.

13 Changes

We update this privacy policy when we use new services or when the law changes. The date at the top shows the current version.
